Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No! Crate Crashers is a fully offline arcade game. You can smash crates and master your trick shots anywhere, without needing Wi-Fi or cellular data.

Is the game free to play?

Yes, you can download and play Crate Crashers for free. Jump right into the explosive, physics-based action.

How does the parachute mechanic work?

When your ball reaches extreme speeds, you can deploy a tactical parachute to slow it down, giving you precise control to navigate tricky obstacles and set up perfect combos.

How does the skill-based shot control work?

Unlike traditional auto-following brick breakers, Crate Crashers demands skill. The ball rests on your platform, and moving too fast will cause it to roll off. You can tap for quick, low-power shots, or hold to charge a powerful, explosive blast.

Is this just a standard brick breaker?

No. Crate Crashers combines classic brick-breaker mechanics with pinball physics and explosive TNT chain reactions. Every smash features dynamic sound and voice effects for a highly satisfying arcade experience.
